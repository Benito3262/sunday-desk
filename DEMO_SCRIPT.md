# Demo script (keep it under 4 minutes)

0:00 — Open the deployed link. Read the line: cash session closed, token still priced.
0:20 — Point at the countdown and one row. Say the on-chain price versus the reference, and the issuer name.
0:45 — Switch basket to Magnificent 7. Open the same ticker if a second issuer is listed. Say which wrapper is further from the reference.
1:20 — Connect wallet. On desktop, use the extension. On phone, show the MetaMask or Trust deep link, or the QR.
1:50 — Tap Plan buy, $10, Simulate only. Leave the quote status, execution mode (SWAP or RFQ), and simulation JSON visible.
2:40 — If you have $5 of USDT and a little BNB: tap Sign and send. Show the wallet prompt, then the BscScan link.
3:20 — Scroll the evidence log. Say this is what the DevEx report is written from: latency, status codes, issuer differences, off-hours behaviour.
3:45 — End on the rule: spot only, BSC mainnet, nothing sends without a signature.
